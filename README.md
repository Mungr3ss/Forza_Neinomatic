# Forza_Neinomatic
xml mods for the forza motorsport & horizon series of games to improve visuals. 
with the aim of making these games more enjoyable to play, reducing the probability of headaches from long 
play sessions by disabling certain artificial post processing effects to give you a clean, crisp and sharp view
while driving, minimising eye strain & reducing blurry visuals.

these changes have enhanced my play sessions and increased immersion letting me focus more on the driving than on trying
to focus on what is ahead of me. it's a been night and day difference for cockpit views across all the games. just removing the blur, lens flares, light rays and bloom made it much easier on the eyes and to use cockpit view.

all these effects might look good on promo media but for gamers it can be intolerable, depending on the amount and intensity of effects.
often we gamers are at the wim of the devs and have to put up with whatever the art director chose to put in post production.
many games do have depth of field, motion blur settings and toggles but sometimes it doesn't cover all instances of these effects so you will still get some forced post effects which are usually depth of field as it's one of the most common effects. it really depends on the game though, some have more or less than others. usually games intended to be "cinematic" have heavy post processing effects.

brief rundown of some common post processing effects: (not all are used in the forza games)

# depth of field (blurs background of a scene, image)
# motion blur (blurs whole or parts of a scene, image with fast movements)
# chromatic aberration (lens distortion effect, often duplicates edges of objects with difference colours)
common distorts the edges of the screen in games.
# lens distortion (can curve the edge of a scene, image giving a fish view)
# vignette (darkened screen corners)
in games it's supposed to focus the players view but in reality it restricts your entire view of the screen by darkening the corners,
it also reduces your field of view (fov) and is not good for certain games like driving and fps where you want to see everything nice and clear.
# bloom (adds extra glow to light sources and can make scenes, image appear blurry)
# AA / TAA (anti aliasing) 
smooths jagged lines, edges of a scene, image by adding various levels of blur, TAA can make games look
super blurry and introduces visual glitches in some game engines, which means you have to use upscaling to make things sharper but in turn adds more blur and ruins the original image further. it's like smearing oil over your screen and then wiping it off and then cleaning the screen with a rough sponge....looks like ass.
# lens flares (if you wear glasses and squint at a lamp, you will see a lens flare)
but in games these are unrealistic and overused, devs treat player characters as walking camera lens in many modern games an go overboard
adding in multiple types of flares in various sizes and configurations. common is sun flares and flares from light sources like street and wall lights in games but also weird flares mixed with lens dirt / dust.

each game where possible the following changes have been performed:

i say where possible as lots of files are inside encrypted zips and not all can be unpacked and repacked as of 2026 on some 
of the newer forza games as the devs keep changing the archive version / method or whatever.

- third person and cockpit lens flares disabled (fh5 has lots of crappy looking cinematic lens flares, these have been disabled)
for example the bit where you transport a vehicle on a truck.
- bloom disabled (makes various light sources appear blurry and unrealistic, can contribute to headaches while playing)
bloom looks like sh*t in fh5 in cockpit view, fake looking glows on everything.
- motion blur completely disabled, making in-game toggles non functional (for example forced motor blur in fm6 apex)
- light rays disabled as they look unrealistic and in games like fm6 apex they heavily obscure driver vision on some weather presets with bright sun.
- rain on camera lens disabled for third person view on games like fh3 as it's annoying. not so much on bumper and hood views.
- depth of field disable in cutscenes (pre-race, replays etc) fm6 apex, fm7, fm23
depth of field is a pet hate of mine, i hate it in every game it's used in, i don't even like using photo mode in games as i prefer old school screenshots without fancy looking post processing altering the original image. game characters as a walking camera lens ruins the immersive experience of games imo. leave that sh*t in the movies thanks!
